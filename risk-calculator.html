How to use this calculator

Pick your pair and account equity, decide how much you're willing to risk (as a percentage or a fixed dollar amount), then type your stop loss and target in pips — everything else updates instantly.

The RR ratio tells you how many dollars you stand to make for every dollar you risk. A ratio of 1:2 or higher is generally considered a favorable setup, since it means you can be wrong more often than right and still be profitable over time.

The position size shown is the exact lot size needed to risk precisely your chosen amount if your stop loss is hit — no more, no less.
